The Problem
🔍

Blind compliance audits

Franchisors still rely on infrequent manual store visits and franchisee self-reporting to assess brand standard adherence across hundreds of locations.

💸

Royalty reporting in the dark

Royalty calculations depend on self-reported revenue from operators. Discrepancies and underreporting go undetected for months — sometimes years.

📊

No network benchmarking

Individual franchise operators have no visibility into how their unit economics stack up against the network average. Franchisors have no systematic way to identify underperformers.

🎓

Training completion is a spreadsheet

Tracking whether a 500-location operator network has completed mandatory certifications is manual, fragmented, and chronically behind schedule.

McDonald's has 40,000 locations. Their compliance team has almost no automated intelligence layer.

Franchise brands operate at massive scale — yet the operational tooling hasn't caught up. FranConnect, the category leader, built its product for workflow management. The intelligence layer — the AI that tells you which location is about to fail an audit, which operator is sandbagging royalty revenue, which unit's EBITDA is 30% below network average — doesn't exist yet.


Kelvyn is that layer. Built natively on real-time data, designed for franchise HQ teams managing hundreds to thousands of operators.

How It Works

Intelligence that acts, not just reports.

01
Connect your data sources
Kelvyn integrates with your POS system, existing franchise management platform, LMS, and accounting tools via API. No rip-and-replace. Works alongside FranConnect, Naranga, and custom stacks.
02
Kelvyn builds your network baseline
Our models ingest 12–24 months of historical data to establish unit-level and network-level baselines across all key metrics — revenue, compliance, training, and operator behavior.
03
Anomalies surface automatically
Deviations from baseline trigger intelligent alerts — prioritized by impact and urgency. Your team sees only what matters, ranked by the dollar value of acting on it.
04
Prescriptive actions, not just data
Kelvyn doesn't just flag problems — it recommends actions. Which operator needs a field visit. Which location needs an audit. Which training module correlates with the performance gap.
